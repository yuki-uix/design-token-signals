CASE 01 / SIG-001 / CANDIDATE RULE

Warmth does not set the reading pace.

The same words, colors, and card shape. Compare body spacing, then check a compact title that leaves the reading surface unchanged.

The brief for this constructed example

An unhurried reading card. Its author-defined body baseline is 16px text, at least 1.65 line height, and 16px between paragraphs. It applies to the body in all three panels at the same viewport. It is not a universal design threshold.

01

Original spacing

Both body values fall below the brief.

FIELD NOTES · 3 MIN READ

A little room
to think

A notebook can be a place to leave a thought before deciding what it means. Write a few lines, close the page, and return when there is something more to say.

Not every idea needs a title or a plan. Some begin as a question from a conversation, a detail noticed on a walk, or a sentence you want to keep.

When you return, read what is already there. Keep the useful parts. Let the rest remain unfinished for a while.

What this comparison can tell us

The CSS and rendered spacing can be checked. Whether the change feels better or helps readers still needs human review. These labels are author interpretations, not output from an audit tool or results of user research.
